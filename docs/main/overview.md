## Overview of the lab's Use Case


You are designing a **Webex AI Agent** for a Webex Financial Group. Your mission is to proactively resolve outstanding fees while maintaining a security-first posture. Guide customers through total or partial payments using secure NovaPay links and provide general information and account or transaction history upon request. Support account inquiries and transaction history and immediately escalate to a human specialist if a customer disputes an outstanding fee or suspects fraud.


### AI Agent Capabilities

- **Build Alex, a voice-based financial services AI agent that helps customers manage fees due.**
- **Verify customers before discussing account-specific information**
- **Explain balances and due dates, and guide customers through full or partial payments using a secure payment link.**
- **Answer general banking questions and provide recent transaction details.**
- **Route transaction disputes or suspected fraud to a human specialist.**
- **Route investment questions to an investment advisor.**
- **Demonstrate conversational support, payment actions, and human handoffs while protecting customer information.**
