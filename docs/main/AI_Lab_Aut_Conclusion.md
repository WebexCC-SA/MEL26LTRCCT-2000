In this lab, you successfully explored the capabilities of the **Webex Autonomous AI Agent** by creating and configuring an AI-powered assistant for a **Self-Banking** experience.

### Key Capabilities Implemented

Designed the virtual agent to:

- Recognise **customer** based on the customer's **NationalId**.
- Customer **Authentication**.
- **Query using Actions** CRM systems
- **Deliver responses** appropriate to the needs of the customer
- **Automate payments - full or partial** including using email communcation for links
- **Update CRM systems** with payment
- **Escalate with context** to human

Configured **actions** to:

- Integrate with a **Customer CRM/Database**.
- Generate **payment links** and provide customers with a **real-time payment status**.

Enabled communication by:

- Sending **payment details via email** from CRM systems.
- Ensuring a **seamless and efficient user experience**.
